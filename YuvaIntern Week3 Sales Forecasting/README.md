## 📝 Task Objective
To forecast sales and visualize trends over time using analytical techniques relevant to eCommerce, using simulated publicly available sales data.

## 🎯 Key Highlights
- **Data:** Monthly sales data (Jan-Dec 2026) with 3-month moving average
- **Methodologies:** Time Series Analysis, Moving Average, Linear Regression, Seasonal Adjustment
- **Forecast:** 6-month projection (Jan-Jun 2027) with seasonal multipliers
- **Formula:** Y = 112,400 + 8,750X (baseline trend)
- **Visualizations:** Line Chart, Bar Chart, Forecast Chart
- **Key Insight:** Q4 (Oct-Dec) accounts for ~38% of annual revenue
