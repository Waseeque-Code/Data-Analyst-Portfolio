## 📋 Project Overview

A comprehensive Olympic Games analytics dashboard analyzing **120+ years** of historical data (1896-2016). This project provides deep insights into athlete demographics, country performance, medal distribution, and historical trends across both Summer and Winter Olympics.

**File:** `athlete_events.csv.xlsx`

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🏆 Country Dominance
- **United States** leads with **18,604** total medals — 2.5x more than second place
- **USA** also tops gold medals with **2,638**
- Top 5 medal-winning nations: USA, France, Great Britain, Italy, Germany

### 👶 Age Factor
- **18-25 age group** secured **150,782 medals** (68% of total)
- Average athlete age: **25.45 years**
- Youngest athlete: Dimitrios Loundras (10 years)
- Oldest athlete: John Quincy Adams Ward (97 years)

### 👩 Women in Olympics
- Female participation grew from **33** (1900) to **6,223** (2016)
- **18,800%** increase over 116 years
- Female-dominated sports: Synchronized Swimming (909), Rhythmic Gymnastics (658), Softball (478)

### 🏅 Medal Distribution
| Medal Type | Count | Percentage |
|------------|-------|------------|
| Gold | 13,369 | 33.6% |
| Silver | 13,108 | 33.0% |
| Bronze | 13,295 | 33.4% |

- **Male athletes:** 28,519 medals (71.7%)
- **Female athletes:** 11,253 medals (28.3%)

### ⚽ Most Popular Sports
| Rank | Sport | Participants |
|------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Athletics | 38,624 |
| 2 | Gymnastics | 26,707 |
| 3 | Swimming | 23,195 |
| 4 | Shooting | 11,448 |
| 5 | Cycling | 10,827 |

### 🌞 Summer vs Winter
| Season | Participants | Percentage |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| Summer | 221,167 | 82% |
| Winter | 48,564 | 18% |

### 📈 Historical Peak
- **1992 Barcelona Olympics** recorded the highest athlete count: **16,413**
